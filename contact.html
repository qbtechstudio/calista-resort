A FEW USEFUL THINGS

Good to know
before you go.

How do I make a reservation?

Send a stay request above or email hello@calistaisland.com. Our team will follow up with availability and the next steps.

How do I arrange transfers to the island?

Share your flight itinerary with the Calista team. We’ll confirm the transfer options that suit your arrival; onward travel arrangements depend on your itinerary.

Can I share a special request before my stay?

Of course. Add a note to your enquiry or email us directly so the team can discuss what may be arranged for your visit.

When is my stay confirmed?

A reservation is confirmed directly by the Calista team after they have discussed availability and arrangements with you. Sending an enquiry alone does not confirm a booking.
